Master Class on Agile Project Manager(McAPM)-

Duration: 12 Houre • Language: English • Certificate: Yes, Industry Verified

This Master Class on Agile Project Manager (McAPM) is designed for professionals seeking to master agile methodologies, project leadership, and adaptive planning in dynamic business environments. The program combines theoretical foundations with practical applications to build comprehensive agile project management competency.

Course Modules

  • 1

    Introduction to Agile Project Management

    Foundations of agile methodology, the Agile Manifesto, and core principles of adaptive project management.

  • 2

    Scrum Framework and Practices

    Scrum roles, events, artifacts, and the application of Scrum in complex project environments.

  • 3

    Agile Planning and Estimation

    User stories, story points, release planning, sprint planning, and velocity-based estimation techniques.

  • 4

    Lean and Kanban for Project Managers

    Lean principles, waste elimination, Kanban boards, work-in-progress limits, and continuous flow management.

  • 5

    Agile Leadership and Team Dynamics

    Servant leadership, building high-performing agile teams, conflict resolution, and facilitating collaboration.

  • 6

    Agile Project Delivery and Metrics

    Delivery frameworks, agile metrics, burndown charts, earned value management in agile, and reporting.

Course Objectives

Develop a comprehensive understanding of agile project management principles and frameworks.

Apply Scrum, Kanban, and Lean methodologies in real-world project scenarios.

Master agile planning, estimation, and delivery techniques for adaptive projects.

Build leadership skills to guide high-performing agile teams effectively.

Learning Outcomes

Earn the McAPM credential, demonstrating mastery of agile project management.

Ability to lead and coach agile teams across diverse organizational contexts.

Capability to design and implement agile project delivery frameworks.

Industry-recognized certification validating agile leadership competency.
