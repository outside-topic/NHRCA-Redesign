Institutional Standards

NHRCA Institutional Standards

The National Human Resource Certification Academy (NHRCA), UK is committed to promoting and upholding high standards in human resource education and professional development worldwide.

Institutions such as training providers, colleges, and universities offering programs in Human Resource Management are expected to maintain structured academic and professional standards to ensure the quality, relevance, and credibility of HR education.

Institutions that successfully align their programs with the NHRCA Institutional Standards may be authorized to use the NHRCA Quality Mark, signifying that their HR programs meet the quality assurance criteria and professional competency standards established by NHRCA.

Individual Standards

NHRCA Individual Standards

The National Human Resource Certification Academy (NHRCA), UK establishes professional standards for the certification of Human Resource professionals.

These standards classify HR certifications based on functional specialization and professional seniority, ensuring that qualifications accurately reflect the competencies and responsibilities associated with different career levels.

Through its structured certification framework, NHRCA promotes clear professional pathways for HR practitioners, supporting the development, recognition, and progression of competent human resource professionals.
