Professional Framework

NHRCA Professional Framework

A structured pathway for HR professionals to develop, practice, lead, and contribute to the advancement of the HR profession.

Introduction

Professional Progression Framework

The NHRCA Professional Framework defines a structured pathway for HR professionals across different professional levels, based on education, professional experience, and focused competency areas.

The framework reflects increasing levels of professional contribution — from HR administration and HR core functions through to strategic HR — guiding professionals as they develop, practice, lead, and contribute to the advancement of the HR profession.

Framework Overview

  • Five professional stages from Learn & Practice to Legendary HR Professional.
  • Education & experience requirements defined per stage.
  • Focused competency areas across HR Administration, HR Core Function, and Strategic HR.
  • Increasing contribution from operational to strategic and advisory levels.
