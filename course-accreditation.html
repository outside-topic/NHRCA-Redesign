Course Accreditation

Course Accreditation

Accreditation that strengthens the value, credibility, and global recognition of your HR courses and professional certifications.

Why Accreditation Matters

Benefit of Accreditation

Credentials awarded by the NHRCA are recognized worldwide, including those awarded to persons by universities, organizations, educational institutions, and training facilities. The most important benefits that an Accredited course will enjoy are:

01

Greater Certificate Value

Accredited courses add more value to the certificates than those that are not accredited.

02

Global Corporate Acceptance

Accredited courses are accepted globally by the corporates.

03

Professional Credibility

Accreditation of any course ensures the credibility of the professional certification.

04

Competency-Based Assessment

Accreditation of any course ensures the professional and perfect assessment of competencies.

05

Course Growth

Accreditation Ensures 10X speed of growth for any course.
